How this estimate is calculated

Annual price × number of licences × years. This example assumes one person per Windows device and the same licence count for both products. The difference between the two totals is the estimated saving. Displayed results are rounded to whole dollars.

Prices are held constant for the comparison period; renewal increases, promotions, negotiated discounts and switching costs are not included. Adobe examples use annual contracts billed monthly; Foxit and Nitro examples are billed annually. Other billing options may have different prices.

Different suppliers may license by user rather than device. Adobe’s named-user licensing assigns access to individual people. Check the supplier’s current plan, terms and price against your own setup. Review the Simple Office Tools features and try your documents before switching.

Before you buy

A few things worth knowing.

One clear annual licence, with the details here when you need them.

Is the licence per person or per device?

Each paid seat covers one Windows device at a time. If your team needs five devices, it needs five seats. You can purchase multiple seats in checkout.

Does it renew automatically?

Yes. The paid licence renews annually unless cancelled before the renewal date. You can cancel to stop future renewals and keep access for the rest of the current paid term. See the payment and cancellation terms.

Do I have to buy when the trial ends?

No. The 30-day trial requires no card and does not automatically become a paid subscription. If you want to keep using the app after the trial, you’ll need to activate a paid licence. You can upgrade inside the app or buy here.

How do I activate my purchase?

After checkout, follow the prompt to open Simple Office Tools. You can also choose Claim a licence in the app, paste the Paddle transaction ID from the purchase-completion page or emailed invoice, then choose Claim licence.

Does the app work offline?

Everyday PDF tools process documents on your computer without uploading them. An internet connection is needed for licence activation and verification, and for downloading updates.

Where can I find the refund policy?

See our refund policy for eligibility, time limits and how to request a refund through Paddle. If you’re unsure about product fit, start with the free trial.
