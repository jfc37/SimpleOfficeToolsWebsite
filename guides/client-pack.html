Combine and organise a client pack

Put a cover, agreement, report and supporting image into one PDF, in the right order.

Before you start: Open Simple Office Tools and have your PDFs ready.

  1. Bring in your PDFs

    Choose Browse files, or drag your files into Documents. Add the PDFs and supporting images you want in the pack, then choose Continue.

  2. Put the pages in order

    In Arrange, use Documents to drag whole files into order. Switch to All pages to move individual pages, rotate sideways pages and clear the checkbox for anything to exclude.

  3. Finish and export

    Choose Continue to edit for optional page numbers or other finishing touches, then choose Continue to review. Choose Export, leave PDF selected, choose Export PDF and save the new file.

  4. Check the result

    Open the exported PDF and check the page order, numbering and visible content before sending it.

Tip: Keep your source files. Export the finished pack as a new PDF.

Common questions

Can I leave pages out?

Yes. In Arrange, exclude pages you do not want in the export, then check the final included count.

Will this change my original PDFs?

Your source files remain separate. The exported pack is a new PDF.
