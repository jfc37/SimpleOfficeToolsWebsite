Make a scanned PDF searchable with OCR

Recognise words in scanned pages so you can search, select, copy and mark up the text without changing the visible scan.

Before you start: Clear, upright pages work best. Recognition uses supported language packs installed in Windows.

  1. Open the scanned PDF

    Add the PDF, arrange the pages you want to keep, then continue to Customise.

  2. Open Recognise text

    Choose Finish, then Recognise text. Select Current page or All included pages.

  3. Choose the language and run OCR

    Open Advanced settings when you need a different installed recognition language, then choose Recognise pages and wait for processing to finish.

  4. Check and export

    Search for important words and try selecting or copying them. Correct your source or rerun recognition if needed, then export the PDF and check it again.

Tip: OCR runs on your computer, but it can miss or misread words and numbers. Check names, totals and reference numbers carefully.

Common questions

Does OCR change how the scan looks?

No. It adds a searchable text layer while leaving the visible scanned page unchanged.

Can I edit the recognised lettering?

OCR makes words searchable and selectable; it does not turn the visible scan into editable PDF text.
