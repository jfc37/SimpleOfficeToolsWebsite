Share an exported PDF by email

Export your finished PDF, then open Windows Share to choose an email app.

Before you start: Finish your edits and open Review & Export. An email app must be installed and configured to appear as a sharing option.

  1. Export the PDF

    Choose Export, select PDF, then choose Export PDF.

  2. Save the file

    Choose a file name and location, then save. Wait for the File exported panel.

  3. Open Share

    Choose Share in the File exported panel. Windows shows the sharing options available on your computer.

  4. Choose an email app and check the draft

    Select an available email app, such as Outlook. The video shows the draft opening. Check the attached PDF, add the recipient and message, and review the email before sending. You can also attach the saved PDF manually in your email app.

Tip: Choose Open PDF to review the exported file before sharing it. Windows sharing options vary with the apps installed on your computer.

Common questions

Why can’t I see my email app?

Windows only shows supported, configured sharing options. Open your email app directly and attach the saved PDF if it is not listed.

Does choosing Share immediately send an email?

No. Share opens the Windows sharing panel. Continue in your chosen app and review the recipient, attachment and message before sending.
