Guides/Add shapes and text comments

Add shapes and text comments

Draw attention to an area and leave a text comment beside it.

Before you start: Open your PDF and continue through Arrange to Customise. Choose Markup.

  1. Draw a shape

    Choose a shape such as Rectangle in Markup. Drag around the area you want to call out.

  2. Set its appearance

    Select the shape to adjust the border colour, thickness and opacity. Move or resize it until it frames the right content.

  3. Add a text comment

    Under Comments, choose Text. Place a comment on the page and enter your wording. Adjust the text colour, font size and position.

  4. Review and export

    Check that comments do not cover content people need to read. Continue to review, export a PDF and inspect the saved result.

Tip: A text comment adds an annotation. To correct the original wording, follow the Edit text guide.

Common questions

Can I remove a shape or comment?

Select the annotation and choose Remove. Review the page again before exporting.

Will a filled rectangle securely hide information?

Use the Redact workflow to permanently remove content. An annotation placed over text does not provide the same result.
