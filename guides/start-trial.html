Guides/Install and start your trial

Install the app and start your trial

Install Simple Office Tools and open your first PDF. Free for 30 days, with no credit card required.

Before you start: Have a Windows x64 computer, an email address and an internet connection for installation and activation.

  1. Download the installer

    Choose Download free 30-day trial on the Download page. Open the stable .appinstaller file when it finishes downloading.

  2. Finish Windows setup

    Follow Windows App Installer, then open Simple Office Tools. Windows 10 needs build 19041 or later.

  3. Start a free trial

    Choose Start a free trial, enter your email address, then choose Start free trial. Optionally name this device and choose Save and continue, or choose Skip. Finally, choose Continue to app.

  4. Open a PDF

    Choose Browse files to add your first PDF and try the workflow on your own document. The trial lasts 30 days and does not automatically become a paid subscription.

Tip: PDF editing, organising and export happen on your computer. Internet is still needed for activation, licence verification and updates.

Common questions

What happens after 30 days?

You need an active paid licence to keep using the app. See the FAQ about trial expiry.

Already have a licence?

Choose Claim a licence, paste the transaction ID from your invoice, then choose Claim licence.
