Guides/Password-protect a PDF

Password-protect an exported PDF

Require a password before someone can open the finished PDF.

Before you start: Choose a strong password and decide how you will share it separately from the PDF.

  1. Finish the document

    Complete your edits and continue to Review & Export.

  2. Choose PDF

    Under Choose a format, keep PDF selected. Password protection applies to PDF output.

  3. Turn on password protection

    Enable Protect with a password, then enter and confirm the password exactly.

  4. Export and test it

    Choose Export PDF, save the file, close it, and reopen it. Confirm that the correct password works and a wrong password does not.

Tip: Send the password by a different channel. Do not place it in the same email as the protected PDF.

Common questions

Can the password be recovered later?

Do not rely on recovery. Keep the password in an approved password manager and retain the unprotected source when your policy allows it.

Can I password-protect Word or Excel output here?

No. This option protects exported PDFs. Use the protection features in Word or Excel for those formats.
