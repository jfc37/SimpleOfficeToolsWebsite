Manual fallback

Activate it inside the app.

If you prefer to do it yourself, these are the exact steps in the Windows app.

  1. Open Simple Office Tools on your computer.
  2. Select the menu button in the top-left corner.
  3. Choose Licence.
  4. Paste the key shown on this page.
  5. Click Activate Existing Licence.

The app still needs an internet connection for activation, even though your PDF work stays local.

Need it later?

Check your Paddle email.

Paddle sends your invoice to the email address used at checkout. The transaction ID on that invoice is the same licence key shown on this page.

Look for an email from help@paddle.com and keep it for future reference.

Activation not working?

Start with the transaction ID.

If your licence key does not appear on this page, the app does not activate, or you need help finding the invoice, include the transaction ID (format: txn_...) when contacting support.

That is the fastest way to trace a purchase and verify the licence.
