3. How to use your licence key in the app

To activate your licence in the Simple Office Tools Windows app:

  • Open the Simple Office Tools app on your computer.
  • Click on the hamburger menu item on the top left corner of the app.
  • Click on the License menu item.
  • Paste your licence key into the Licence key field.
  • Select Activate Existing Licence to complete activation.

Once activated, your device will be registered to your licence.

For step-by-step instructions and screenshots, please see our FAQ page.

Having trouble? Double-check that you copied the entire key with no extra spaces, and that your device has an active internet connection during activation.

4. Where to find your licence key later

Keep that email somewhere safe so you can refer to it in future.
